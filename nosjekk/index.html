Norwegian number checks, as Google Sheets formulas

=NO_FNR_VALID(A2), =NO_ORGNR_VALID(B2:B500) and =NO_KID_VALID(C2) check the MOD10/MOD11 control digits offline. Pro creates KID numbers, converts accounts to IBAN and counts Norwegian working days.

Install from Google Workspace Marketplace (in review) 14-day Pro trial, then $4/month or $32/year

Features

Personal numbers
Fødselsnummer, D-nummer, H-nummer and synthetic test numbers: both control digits plus the date. Pro also gives the date of birth, gender and type.
Business and bank
Organisasjonsnummer and VAT format, 11-digit kontonummer, and IBAN in Pro.
KID
Validate MOD10 and MOD11 KID references, or create them from customer and invoice numbers in Pro.
Virkedager (Pro)
Norwegian public holidays, working days between two dates, and adding working days to a date.

Help

Install the add-on, then use Extensions › NO-Sjekk › Insert example sheet. Every validator accepts a range and returns TRUE/FALSE for each cell.

NO-Sjekk validates format and check digits only. It does not look anything up in Brønnøysundregistrene or Folkeregisteret.

The add-on uses only the current-spreadsheet permission (spreadsheets.currentonly) and sends no data anywhere. Support: drudleybot+nosjekk@mail.grokbot.com
