3. Data sent to third parties

  • Sveriges riksbank (api.riksbank.se) and, as a fallback, the European Central Bank (data-api.ecb.europa.eu): only a currency code and a date range. No spreadsheet content or personal data.
  • European Commission VIES (ec.europa.eu): the VAT number you pass to VAT_VALID or the batch tool. We read only the valid/not-valid flag from the response; trader names and addresses returned by VIES are discarded and never stored or shown.
  • Polar Software Inc. (api.polar.sh), our Merchant of Record: your licence key, to check whether your subscription is active. Purchases are made on Polar's checkout, and Polar processes your name, e-mail and payment details under its own privacy policy. We receive order and customer details from Polar to provide support and meet accounting obligations.

Personal identity numbers (personnummer), organisation numbers, bank accounts, OCR, Bankgiro and Plusgiro numbers are validated entirely inside Google Apps Script and are never sent to any external service.

4. Data stored

  • In your spreadsheet's add-on storage (Document Properties): your licence key (if you activate one), its last validation status and the start date of your free trial.
  • In your Google account's add-on storage (User Properties): your licence key, so you can apply it to other spreadsheets, and the start date of your free trial, so the 14-day trial is used once per account.
  • In Google's temporary script cache (max 6 hours): downloaded exchange rates, VAT-number validity results (the VAT number and true/false) and licence validation results keyed by a one-way hash of the key.
  • In Google's Apps Script execution logs: when a Kronkalkyl formula returns an error, Google records the error message for the add-on's developer. For invalid input the message can include the value you entered (for example an invalid number). These logs stay within Google Cloud, are used only to diagnose faults, and are deleted automatically under Google's standard log retention.

Apart from the values listed in section 3, no spreadsheet data leaves Google.

We run no servers of our own that receive your spreadsheet data. We do not use analytics or advertising trackers in the add-on. Uninstalling the add-on stops all processing; the cache expires within 6 hours, and "Remove licence" in the sidebar deletes the licence key and its status from that spreadsheet. The copy saved in your Google account's add-on storage is held only by Google for your account and is never sent to us; e-mail drudleybot+kronkalkyl@mail.grokbot.com if you need help removing it.
