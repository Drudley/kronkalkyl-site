Sammanfattning på svenska: Rutakalkyl räknar momsfält i ditt Google Kalkylark. Belopp och transaktioner lämnar inte kalkylarket. Endast licensnyckeln skickas till Polar om du aktiverar Pro. 1. Who we are

Rutakalkyl ("the add-on") is a Google Sheets™ Editor add-on provided by Niklas Bergström, Tråggränd 20, 906 26 Umeå, Sweden ("we"). Contact: drudleybot+rutakalkyl@mail.grokbot.com.

2. What the add-on can access

The add-on requests only these non-restricted Google scopes:

  • The current spreadsheet (spreadsheets.currentonly), so it can create the ready-made momsdeklaration template and read the amounts in the sheet you are using. The add-on cannot open any other file in your Google Drive.
  • The add-on user interface (script.container.ui), so it can show the sidebar.
  • External requests (script.external_request), solely to validate a Polar licence key if you activate Pro.

We do not request access to your Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, calendar or profile. Transaction amounts, descriptions and other cell values are processed only inside Google Sheets™ and are never sent to us or to Polar.

3. Data sent to third parties

  • Polar Software Inc. (api.polar.sh), our Merchant of Record: your licence key, to check whether your subscription is active. Purchases are made on Polar's checkout, and Polar processes your name, e-mail and payment details under its own privacy policy. We receive order and customer details from Polar to provide support and meet accounting obligations.

Transaction amounts, descriptions and other cell values are calculated entirely inside Google Apps Script and are never sent to any external service.

4. Data stored

  • In your spreadsheet's add-on storage (Document Properties): your licence key (if you activate one), its last validation status and the start date of your free trial.
  • In your Google account's add-on storage (User Properties): your licence key, so you can apply it to other spreadsheets, and the start date of your free trial, so the 14-day trial is used once per account.
  • In Google's temporary script cache (max 6 hours): licence validation results keyed by a one-way hash of the key.
  • In Google's Apps Script execution logs: when a Rutakalkyl formula returns an error, Google records the error message for the add-on's developer. For invalid input the message can include the value you entered. These logs stay within Google Cloud, are used only to diagnose faults, and are deleted automatically under Google's standard log retention.

Apart from the licence key sent to Polar, no spreadsheet data leaves Google.

We run no servers of our own that receive your spreadsheet data. We do not use analytics or advertising trackers in the add-on. Uninstalling the add-on stops all processing; the cache expires within 6 hours, and "Remove licence" in the sidebar deletes the licence key and its status from that spreadsheet. The copy saved in your Google account's add-on storage is held only by Google for your account and is never sent to us; e-mail drudleybot+rutakalkyl@mail.grokbot.com if you need help removing it.

5. How we protect your data

  • Encryption in transit: every request the add-on makes to an external service (Polar) uses HTTPS/TLS. The add-on can only contact api.polar.sh; the list is fixed in the add-on's manifest (URL allowlist) and enforced by Google Apps Script.
  • Encryption at rest and isolation: the add-on runs entirely inside Google Apps Script. Everything it stores (add-on properties, cache and logs, see section 4) stays on Google's infrastructure, which encrypts data at rest. Add-on properties are kept per spreadsheet or per Google account, so other users cannot read them; the shared cache holds only hashed licence validation results.
  • Sensitive data stays local: transaction amounts, descriptions and other cell values are processed in memory during the formula call and are never sent outside Google, never written to the cache and never stored by us. The only exception is that an invalid value can appear in an error message in Google's execution logs, as described in section 4.
  • Minimum access: the add-on requests only the narrow permissions in section 2. It can access only the spreadsheet you use it in; it has no access to your other files, Gmail or contacts.
  • Minimised identifiers: licence validation results are cached under a one-way hash of the key, not the key itself.
  • Restricted access: only the developer can access the add-on's Google Cloud project, execution logs and the Polar customer records, via password-protected accounts. We have no servers or databases of our own that hold your spreadsheet data.
  • Incidents: if a personal data breach affecting you occurs, we will notify the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY) within 72 hours where required and inform affected users without undue delay.
