Danish CPR, CVR and FI-kort checks, as Google Sheets formulas

=DK_CVR_VALID(A2), =DK_CPR_VALID(B2) and =DK_FIK_VALID(C2). Pro creates +71/+75/+04 payment lines and IBANs and counts bankdage.

Install from Google Workspace Marketplace (in review) 14-day Pro trial, then $4/month or $32/year

Features

CPR
Date and century digit, with modulus 11 optional because not every CPR number issued since 2007 satisfies it.
CVR and moms
CVR modulus 11 and the DK momsnummer format.
FI-kort (Pro)
Payment lines with the modulus 10 check digit for +71, +75 and +04.
Bank days (Pro)
Helligdage without store bededag from 2024, plus the banks' extra closing days.

Help

Use Extensions › DK-Tjek › Insert example sheet to get started. Every validator accepts a range.

DK-Tjek validates format and check digits only. It does not query CPR or CVR.

The add-on uses only the current-spreadsheet permission (spreadsheets.currentonly) and sends no data anywhere. Support: drudleybot+dktjek@mail.grokbot.com
