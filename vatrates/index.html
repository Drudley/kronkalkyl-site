The right EU VAT rate for the invoice date, in one formula

=EU_VAT_RATE("FI"; A2) gives 24 on 31 Aug 2024 and 25.5 from 1 Sep 2024. Every EU-27 standard-rate change since 2020 is built in, and Pro adds reduced rates and a full table.

Install from Google Workspace Marketplace (in review) 14-day Pro trial, then $3/month or $24/year

Features

Dated standard rates
FI, EE, SK, RO, plus the temporary DE, IE and LU cuts, all handled by the invoice date.
Net ↔ gross
=EU_VAT_GROSS and =EU_VAT_NET, on single cells or ranges.
Reduced rates (Pro)
Reduced, super-reduced and parking rates by name, including Lithuania's 2026 reform.
Offline
A built-in table, so there are no API keys, no quotas and no network requests.

Help

Use Extensions › EU VAT Rates › Insert price-list example to get started.

Rates are built in. When a country changes its rate, a new add-on version is published. Territorial variants are not included, and the rate for a specific product is your responsibility.

The add-on uses only the current-spreadsheet permission (spreadsheets.currentonly) and sends no data anywhere. Support: drudleybot+vatrates@mail.grokbot.com
