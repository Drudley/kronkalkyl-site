1. Who we are

Avskrivning ("the add-on") is a Google Sheets™ Editor add-on provided by Niklas Bergström, Tråggränd 20, 906 26 Umeå, Sweden ("we"). Contact: drudleybot+avskrivning@mail.grokbot.com.

2. What the add-on can access

The add-on requests a single, non-sensitive Google scope: the current spreadsheet (spreadsheets.currentonly). It uses it to insert the templates you choose from the add-on menu into new tabs and to store your trial or licence status in a hidden settings tab. It cannot open any other file in your Google Drive and has no access to Gmail, contacts, calendar or your profile. Custom functions run without any authorisation and only see the arguments you pass to them.

3. Data sent to third parties

None. The add-on makes no network requests: all calculations use data built into the add-on, and licence keys are checked offline with a digital signature. If you buy Pro, the purchase happens on Polar's checkout (Polar Software Inc., Merchant of Record), which processes your name, e-mail and payment details under its own privacy policy; we receive order details from Polar to deliver your licence key and for bookkeeping.

4. Data stored

  • In your spreadsheet (hidden tab "Avskrivning – settings"): the trial start time or the licence key you entered.
  • In your Google account's add-on storage (User Properties): the date you first started the free trial, so the 14-day trial is used once per account.
  • In Google's Apps Script execution logs: if a formula returns an error, Google may log the error message (which can include the value you entered) for the developer. Logs stay within Google Cloud and expire under Google's standard retention.

We run no servers that receive spreadsheet data, and the add-on contains no analytics or advertising trackers. Deleting the settings tab or uninstalling the add-on removes what is stored in your spreadsheet.

5. How we protect your data

  • Minimum access: one non-sensitive scope, limited to the open spreadsheet.
  • No transfer: no external requests, so cell values never leave Google.
  • Encryption at rest: everything the add-on stores stays on Google's infrastructure, which encrypts data at rest.
  • Restricted access: only the developer can access the add-on's Google Cloud project and the Polar customer records, via password-protected accounts.
  • Incidents: if a personal data breach affecting you occurs, we will notify the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY) within 72 hours where required and inform affected users without undue delay.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy

Avskrivning's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell user data, do not use it for advertising, do not use it to train AI/ML models, and do not allow humans to read it except with your explicit consent for support, for security purposes, or to comply with law.

7. Legal basis and your rights (GDPR)

We process customer data to perform our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and to meet bookkeeping obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)). You may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction or portability, and object to processing, by e-mailing drudleybot+avskrivning@mail.grokbot.com. You may lodge a complaint with IMY, imy.se.

8. Retention

Customer and order records are kept as long as required by the Swedish Bookkeeping Act (currently 7 years).
