Finnish ID and payment-reference checks, as Google Sheets formulas

=FI_HETU_VALID(A2) handles the 2023 century signs. =FI_REF_VALID(B2) checks viitenumero and RF references. Pro creates references, RF and the 54-digit virtuaaliviivakoodi, and counts pankkipäivät.

Install from Google Workspace Marketplace (in review) 14-day Pro trial, then $4/month or $32/year

Features

Henkilötunnus
Date, check character and every century sign: +, -, Y–U and A–F.
Business and bank
Y-tunnus, ALV-numero (Pro) and IBAN for every country.
Invoices (Pro)
Viitenumero from an invoice number, RF creditor reference and the virtual bank barcode, versions 4 and 5.
Bank days (Pro)
Finnish holidays including juhannus and pyhäinpäivä, and bank-day counting.

Help

Use Extensions › FI-Tarkistus › Insert example sheet to get started. Every validator accepts a range.

FI-Tarkistus validates format and check digits only. It does not query DVV, PRH or YTJ.

The add-on uses only the current-spreadsheet permission (spreadsheets.currentonly) and sends no data anywhere. Support: drudleybot+fitarkistus@mail.grokbot.com
